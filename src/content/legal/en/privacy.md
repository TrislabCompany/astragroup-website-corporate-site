## Controller

The controller of personal data is ASTRA GROUP d.o.o., Ulica 15. maja 2, 6000 Koper - Capodistria, Slovenia. The contact details are on the [Contact](/en/contact) page.

## Visiting the site

The site sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, runs no JavaScript and loads nothing from other providers.
The map is only a link to Google Maps: Google receives data only when you open the link, and handles it under its own rules.

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. as our processor.
On every visit it logs technical data (IP address, time, the page requested and the browser type) needed to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse.
The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running the site securely (Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation).
Cloudflare may also process the data outside the European Union, under the standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and keeps it only as long as this purpose needs.

## When you call or write to us

When you call or write to us, we use your name, your contact details and your message to answer your enquiry and prepare an offer (Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation).
We keep the data as long as the enquiry needs it, or, if we sign a contract, as long as the law requires.

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing, to data portability and to object.
To exercise your rights, write to the address on the [Contact](/en/contact) page.
You can complain to the Slovenian Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec, www.ip-rs.si).
