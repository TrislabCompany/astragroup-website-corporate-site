---
title: "Accessibility statement"
description: "How accessible the Astra Group website is and how to report a problem."
---

We want everyone to be able to use this site.
It is built to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

## How we check it

With every change we check the site automatically with axe-core, on a desktop screen and at a screen width of 320 pixels.
The pages work without JavaScript, the text has enough contrast, every photo that carries meaning has a description, and you can move through the site with a keyboard.

## Known limitations

We know of no part of the site that is not accessible.
The sites fasadanakljuc.si and strehanakljuc.si, which some links lead to, have their own statements.

## Report a problem

If any part of the site is hard to use, write to us or call us; the details are on the [Contact](/en/contact) page.
We will answer and fix the problem as soon as we can.
