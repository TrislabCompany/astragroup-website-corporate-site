## Who we are

Astra Group is based in Koper, in the Coastal-Karst region of Slovenia, where it does most of its work.
We build and renovate houses and commercial buildings from the foundations to turnkey, so the client has one point of contact for the whole house.

## How we work

We invest in our people's skills, in modern equipment and in new ways of working.
We aim to do good work, on the agreed schedule, with careful use of materials and energy.
