    - question: "When is it time to renovate a roof?"

We build new roofs and renovate old ones, from the roof structure and insulation to the covering you choose.

A roof protects the house from rain, sun and the bora, and a poorly insulated roof lets a lot of heat escape. So when we renovate a roof we always check the structure and the insulation too, not just the covering.
