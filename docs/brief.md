# astragroup.si: build brief

For the development team. Generated by `handoff-plan` from the analysis in tl-product-ideas and copied to the product repository as `docs/brief.md` by `handoff-apply`. The product repository is public: no client contacts, budget, prices, offers or personal names.

## What to build

The corporate site of Astra Group, a construction and renovation company based in the Coastal-Karst region of Slovenia, also working in Friuli Venezia Giulia (Italy) and in Istria and Kvarner (Croatia): residential and commercial construction (new builds, extensions, floor additions, reconstructions), facades, roofing, tiling, courtyards, and renovations and adaptations, "from the foundations to turnkey". It is rebuilt on `tl-template-website` in the repository `astragroup-website-corporate-site` and replaces today's Angular single-page app.

Its readers are homeowners, who want to find the service and call quickly, and business clients and investors, who want the company details and proof of work. The site builds trust: it presents the company, what it does and its projects, and leads to the contact page. There is no sign-in, no stored data and no form: contact is an e-mail link and a phone link.

Astra Group has two extension sites, on their own domains and in their own repositories, about facades and roofs. This site's facade and roofing pages link to them; they share no look with this site.

## Design direction

The template is only the frame (build, checks, languages, legal pages, hosting). The look is the site's own, in its `src/` (components, layouts, pages, styles). A site that only went through init still looks like the template's sample.

- **Feel:** a solid regional builder: confident, orderly and practical. Large photos of the company's real work, a dark navy header, a simple grid, short texts that answer plain questions (what we do, who we are, where we work, our projects). Not a glossy group site.
- **Colours:** the client keeps today's palette; the contrast is fixed:
  - navy `#1A1B3B` for the header, the footer, dark sections, headings and text links (16.6:1 on white, 15.2:1 on `#F5F5F5`);
  - amber `#FFB800` only as a surface: buttons, highlights and bands, always with navy text (9.6:1). Amber is never used for text on a light background (1.7:1 on white). On navy, amber text and lines pass (9.6:1);
  - text `#333333` (12.6:1 on white), secondary text `#666666` (5.7:1 on white, 5.3:1 on `#F5F5F5`);
  - light sections `#F5F5F5`, the page background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Type:** Roboto, as today, self-hosted (no request to Google Fonts); a heavier weight for headings. No icon font: icons as inline SVG.
- **Photos:**
  - the company's own project photos from today's site first, resized; the 2–3 MB originals never reach a page;
  - a service page may show a stock photo whose licence allows commercial use without attribution; a project page shows only that project's real photos;
  - full-bleed in the home hero, as cards with the text on a dark navy gradient, and as a gallery on each project page;
  - AVIF at a few widths, lazy below the first screen, inside the page-weight budget.
- **Home page, top to bottom:**
  1. the hero: a large photo of a finished house, the slogan ("Sanjam gradimo dom!" in Slovenian; its translation in each language), one sentence on the region and "from the foundations to turnkey", and two buttons ("Our services", "Contact us"); the dark header lies over it;
  2. the six service groups as cards with a photo and a one-line description, each leading to its page;
  3. how a job runs, in a few numbered steps, from the first visit to the handover (from today's texts);
  4. the projects as cards with the place and the type of work;
  5. the regions the company works in, by country: the Slovenian coast and the Karst, Friuli Venezia Giulia in Italy, and Istria and Kvarner in Croatia, in a short band;
  6. contact: the e-mail and phone links, and a Google Maps link, on an amber band with navy text;
  7. the footer with the company details and the legal pages.
- **Other pages:**
  - About is the company's story, told as a short chronicle (the founding, the region, the way of working), then the vision, mission, values and goals as a short list, not four equal blocks;
  - each service page opens with a photo head, then what is included (the four items of the group), how it runs, the related projects, and a call to the contact page; the facade and roofing pages follow libelagroup.si (*Fasade*, *Krovstvo*): the kinds of facade or roof covering, what the price depends on, the steps of the work and the common questions, all general facts of the trade with no price or promise; they send the reader to the extension site (fasadanakljuc.si, strehanakljuc.si) from the head and from a band;
  - the services page shows what each group includes on its card, the two extension sites and how a job runs;
  - the projects page lists today's three projects; each project page has the place, the type of work, a short text and its gallery;
  - Contact has the e-mail and phone links, the address with a Google Maps link, and the company details.
- **The site's own components:** the service card, the numbered step list, the project card (photo, place, type), the project gallery (opens without a script library), the contact band, the link card to an extension site.
- **Navigation:** Home, About, Services (the six groups below it), Projects, Contact. On a phone the links fold into a menu that opens without JavaScript, and a call button stays at hand. A language switch in the header with the four languages.
- **From the reference sites:**
  - cgp.si: the short menu and the home-page order (hero with one slogan and two buttons, service blocks, reference cards with name and place); never an empty card;
  - kolektorgradbenistvo.si: one page per project with its category and place; menu labels that answer plain questions;
  - habaugroup.com: large site photos and a dark header with a white logo;
  - ir-gruppe.com: the about page as a short story with photos.
- **Design rules,** checked by `pnpm test` where a machine can:
  - a photo shows at most once on a page;
  - every photo has alternative text in every language; a decorative photo has empty alternative text;
  - a project shows only its own real photos; a stock photo never stands for a project or a place;
  - a photo from the earlier site is marked "to check before production" until it is known that the company may publish it;
  - a photo's credit is shown where its licence asks for one; stock photos are chosen only with licences that need none;
  - text on a photo sits on a dark gradient, and the element under it has a dark background colour of its own;
  - no placeholder text (sample text, lorem ipsum, TBD, TODO) reaches any page, in any language, the legal pages included;
  - every page is in its own language, and every language has the same pages and sections;
  - no figure, partner, certificate or claim that the company has not confirmed; a fact that can't be checked is left out;
  - every menu link leads to a finished page in the site's design;
  - no `style` attributes in the HTML; long words in headings break at 320px.

## Pages or modules

Several pages, built from the template's sections (`page-sections`), each in four languages:

- **Home** (see the design direction).
- **About:** the company story, the region, vision, mission, values and goals.
- **Services:** an overview, and one page per service group (`content-collections`, list `services`): construction (new builds, extensions, floor additions, reconstructions), facades, roofing, tiling, courtyards, and renovations and adaptations. Each with what is included (four items), how it runs and a call to contact.
- **Projects:** a list with a page per project (`content-collections`, list `projects`; `references`): today's three projects, each with its place, type of work, a short text and its photos (`photo-gallery`). No more are planned.
- **Contact** (`contact-without-backend`, `location-map` as a link).
- **Imprint, privacy policy, general terms and accessibility statement** (`legal-pages`). Today's privacy text says the site uses cookies and tracking, which it doesn't: the new texts say what the site really does.

## Languages

Slovenian (default, opens first), Italian, Croatian and English, on every page. Each language has its own URL, in its own words (`/sl/storitve/fasade`, `/it/servizi/facciate`, `/en/services/facades`), with `hreflang` and `x-default`, and the sitemap lists every language (`multi-language`). Today's site has all four in its translation files, but on one URL. Trislab rewrites every text from today's texts in all four languages; the translations are reviewed before production. The construction terms follow each language's usage in the region (Koper/Capodistria, Istria).

## Domains and environments

- **Main address:** astragroup.si. www.astragroup.si and http redirect to https://astragroup.si permanently at the Cloudflare edge.
- **Environments** on Cloudflare Pages: `dev` at dev.astragroup.si (every merge to `main`) and `staging` at staging.astragroup.si (release tags), both behind Cloudflare Access; `production` at production.astragroup.si behind sign-in until the cutover, then at astragroup.si, public. A release is promoted from `staging` after the release approver's sign-off. The steps are in the template's `docs/deployment.md`.
- **Edge:** astragroup.si becomes a new zone in Trislab's Cloudflare account (tl-cloud-infrastructure `global/edge/records/astragroup.si/`). Today the site and the mail are on a shared host with its own nameservers. Every record in use today is copied into the zone before the nameservers move, so the old site and the mail keep working. At the cutover the site records to the old host go; the mail records stay. The zone is signed (DNSSEC) and blocks AI crawlers at the edge like the other zones.
- **Search Console** (`search-console`): the domain property `sc-domain:astragroup.si`, owned by Trislab's role account. Trislab creates it and adds its verification record with the key `google` in tl-cloud-infrastructure `global/edge/records/astragroup.si/astragroup.si.yaml`, linked in `catalog/domains.yaml`; keep the record through the cutover. The sitemap is `https://astragroup.si/sitemap-index.xml`: Trislab submits it after the cutover and checks the site in Search Console 4–6 weeks later.

## Integrations and outside services

- **Cloudflare Web Analytics:** cookieless visitor statistics on production only (`web_analytics: true`, the template's `docs/analytics.md`). The privacy policy names it.
- **Google Maps:** a plain link on the Contact page only; nothing is embedded.
- **The extension sites:** plain links from the facade and roofing pages.
- Everything else is self-hosted (fonts, icons, images). The Content-Security-Policy stays at the template's default plus what analytics needs.

## Quality baseline

The template's whole baseline, no exceptions: `responsive-layout` (mobile first, no sideways scrolling at 320px), `accessibility` (WCAG 2.1 AA and a statement), `performance-budget`, `page-weight-budget`, `image-optimisation`, `caching`, `compression`, `security-headers`, `error-pages`. Uptime alerts come when the platform has them. Browsers: current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every page exists in all four languages, with `hreflang`, a canonical address and a sitemap entry; Slovenian opens at the root.
- `ci` is green: the build, the page-weight budget, the browser check (no sideways scrolling at 320px, axe-core), the claims check, the design check (no photo twice on a page, no placeholder text) and the `security.txt` check.
- The site sets no cookies and loads nothing from another origin but Cloudflare Web Analytics on production.
- Today's routes, www.astragroup.si and http redirect permanently (301) to the new pages; an unknown address answers 404.
- Google's Rich Results Test reads the company's `LocalBusiness` data.
- SEO (owner: the development team, unless named): the Lighthouse SEO score passes in `ci`; every page has a title, a description and one share image with alt text in all four languages; dev, staging and production.astragroup.si stay behind sign-in and out of search. At promotion (owner: the release approver): the go-live check on production.astragroup.si. After the cutover (owner: Trislab): the sitemap is submitted in Search Console, and the old addresses show as redirected, not as errors.
- The design check: the release approver compares staging with this brief's design direction (palette and contrast, type, the home page's sections in order, the site's own components); nothing looks like the template's sample or like the two extension sites.
- Before production: every fact has been confirmed by the company, the photos from the earlier site are cleared, the translations are reviewed, the legal pages are in place in every language, and the release approver accepts staging.
- After the nameservers move and after the cutover: mail to the domain still arrives.
